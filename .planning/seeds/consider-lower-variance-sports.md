title: Consider Lower-Variance Sports
trigger_condition: If the Premier League's natural variance (and high draw rate) prevents the model from reaching the desired absolute hit rate (e.g., 70%+), evaluate a pivot to a sport with fewer upsets and no draws.

Sports like Tennis or Basketball have significantly fewer upsets and no draws, meaning the heavy favorites win at a much higher frequency than in football. If the football model's accuracy hits a hard ceiling due to the nature of the sport, pivot to one of these markets to achieve the high hit rate goal.
