---
title: High Hit Rate vs Market Edge
date: 2026-10-04
context: Ideation session clarifying the project's definition of "winning"
---

The core motivation for this project is a high frequency of winning predictions (even on heavy favorites with short odds), rather than hunting for inefficient market pricing or a long-term betting edge. 

This means the Premier League scaffold is perfectly suitable, provided we focus the modeling effort on isolating matches with extremely high win probabilities (e.g., 75%+). Finding hidden value in 52% probability matches is not the objective.
